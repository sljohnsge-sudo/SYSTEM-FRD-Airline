1.0 INTRODUCTION 
 
This Functional Requirements Document (FRD) serves as a comprehensive extension to the proposal 
document, providing detailed specifications and additional requirements that build upon the features 
outlined in the proposal.  
 
2.0 BUSINESS REQUIREMENTS OVERVIEW 
 
The B2B Amadeus system aims to provide a seamless online booking experience for flights, hotels, 
packages, transfers, car rentals, and travel insurance. It integrates with multiple GDS systems, offers cross-
selling capabilities, and provides administrative control via a centralized panel. This project fulfills the need 
for efficient travel service booking, package management, contract distribution, and agent reporting.

4.0 NON-FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS 
 
• Performance: Ensure high system performance with minimal downtime. 
• Scalability: Support increasing numbers of agents and transactions. 
• Security: Implement robust authentication and data protection. 
• Usability: Provide intuitive and user-friendly interfaces. 
• Payment Methods: Offer diverse and easy-to-use payment options. 
• Quick Reissuing: Enable fast and efficient ticket reissuing. 
• Customer Support: Provide 24/7 customer assistance. 
 
 
 
5.0 ASSUMPTIONS AND CONSTRAINTS 
 
• Integration with third -party suppliers and GDS is dependent on their API availability and 
compatibility. 
• Multi-currency functionality relies on real-time exchange rate updates.

• TO/GP: Turnover/Gross Profit. 
• GDS: Global Distribution System.
